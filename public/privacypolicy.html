Your records. Your choice.

Daywell 1.2 is an open-source personal wellness tracker. No analytics, advertising, or required account.

Health Connect access

Daywell requests read-only access to steps, sleep and weight when you choose Connect & sync. It imports the last seven days to show your daily activity and recent measurements. No other health permissions are requested. Sync is manual. Permissions can be revoked in Android Health Connect settings. Imported records can be removed in Daywell’s Connected health screen.

Storage

Records are held in an app-private SQLite database on your device. The database is not separately encrypted. Android device encryption and your lock screen provide device protection. Android cloud backup is disabled. Exported JSON backups contain readable personal records; keep them private. Copies saved outside Daywell are not removed when you clear its data.

In-app camera

Camera access is requested only when you open the in-app preview. The preview supports taking a still image. No microphone or location is needed. You can use the system camera or choose an existing image instead.

Meal photos

Camera and selected gallery images are resized, re-encoded and stored privately on your device. The app does not save them to the public gallery. Original EXIF metadata is not retained in the processed photo. Photos are included in exported backups. Only pressing the AI estimate button sends the selected photo and your plate description to the chosen provider. Saving a photo-only meal uploads nothing. Deleting all local data removes the private photo directory. Deleting a single meal removes its diary reference; an unused private image file can remain until all local data is cleared.

Optional AI

Offline summaries send no data. When you enable a provider and send a message, Daywell sends your profile, saved memory, recent food and workout summaries, check-ins, and conversation context to OpenAI. Imported health records and weight measurements are excluded unless you separately enable health sharing. API keys and OAuth credentials are encrypted using Android Keystore and excluded from backups. Provider terms, retention and billing apply separately. ChatGPT sign-in does not import ChatGPT memory or conversations.

Diet and training plans

When you request a GPT plan, Daywell sends the relevant saved profile, preferences, recent summaries, equipment and active-session information to your chosen provider. Plans are drafts, not medical advice. You review before applying; completed training sets are preserved. Saving a meal plan does not log food as eaten.

Food lookup

Barcode lookup sends the entered product barcode to Open Food Facts. The provider can receive your IP address. Imported product data is credited to Open Food Facts and licensed under ODbL.

Deletion and access

You can export, restore or delete local records in Settings. Disconnecting AI removes local credentials; revoke application authorization in your OpenAI account to remove provider-side access. Deleting the app or its data may make records unrecoverable without your backup.

General wellness

Daywell does not diagnose conditions or provide medical treatment. Food estimates and training templates require your judgment. No data is sold or used for advertising.
